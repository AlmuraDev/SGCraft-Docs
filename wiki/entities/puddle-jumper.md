# Puddle jumper

The puddle jumper is a small ship that seats ten and flies through a stargate with its crew aboard. You carry it as an item and put it down as an entity.

```infobox
Puddle jumper
ID: sgcraft:puddle_jumper
Image: scenes/puddle-jumper-night.jpg
Category: Vehicles
Type: Entity and item
Size: 2.75 wide, 2.5 tall, 7 long
Health: Breaks when its damage passes 40 (each hit adds twice its damage, and the count falls by 1 a tick)
Speed: 11 m/s, 40 m/s with turbo, 4 m/s in reverse
Seats: 10 players
Weapons: 6 drones
Stackable: No
Power: None, the drive is a switch
```

## Obtaining

### Crafting

```recipe
sgcraft:puddle_jumper
```

## Usage

1. Use the jumper item on a block face. It goes down beside that face, facing the way you face. It won't go down where it doesn't fit, nose to tail.
2. Use it to lower its cargo door and walk in, or use it again to raise the door.
3. Sit at the helm, press **O** to switch the drive on, and fly. See [Flying](../puddle-jumper.md#flying).

To pick it up, sneak and use it with an empty hand while nobody is aboard. A name tag renames it.

## Behavior

- It flies only while its drive is on and its cargo door is up. With the drive off, it falls.
- It has no owner. Anyone can board, fly, pick up or rename it.
- It goes through gates with its riders, who all need the right to use both gates. See [Through a gate](../puddle-jumper.md#through-a-gate).
- The cabin keeps out water, rain, snow, fire and lava. See [The cabin](../puddle-jumper.md#the-cabin).
- It takes a fifth of the damage a boat would, and nothing aboard can hurt it.

## Drops

| How | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Picked up (sneak and use with an empty hand) | The puddle jumper item, with its name |
| Destroyed | The puddle jumper item |
| Hit by a creative player | Nothing |

A jumper that has been picked up or destroyed comes back with six drones and its drive off.
